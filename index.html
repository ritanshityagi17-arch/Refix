Circular Decision Matrix

The Three Pathways: Repair, Reuse, Replace

We prioritize longevity, circularity, and environmental stewardship, while balancing realistic economics and personal safety.

⇣ DROP CARD HERE TO EVALUATE REPAIR
🔧

1. Repair

Fix It First • Restore Function

The highest priority in the circular economy. Restores original utility using simple hand tools, adhesives, or spare parts.

  • ✓ Repair cost < 50% of new replacement price
  • ✓ Spare parts or standard adhesives available
  • ✓ No structural hazard or toxic degradation
  • ✓ Maximizes carbon, water, and raw material preservation
⇣ DROP CARD HERE TO EVALUATE REUSE
🔄

2. Reuse & Repurpose

Give A Second Life • Upcycle

When primary function is impaired or uneconomical to restore, transform durable raw materials into creative, useful new items.

  • ✓ Primary repair cost > 65% of original value
  • ✓ Structural materials (wood, denim, metal) are sound
  • ✓ Donate to community libraries or reassign utility
  • ✓ Diverts 100% of the object from municipal landfills
⇣ DROP CARD HERE TO EVALUATE REPLACE
🛑

3. Replace Responsibly

Certified Recycling • Safety Veto

When an item compromises safety (flaking PTFE Teflon, cracked helmet shock foam) or is irreparably degraded. Pair with certified recycling.

  • ✓ Critical safety hazards veto dangerous DIY repairs
  • ✓ Repair costs exceed value with negligible longevity
  • ✓ Reclaim metals and plastics via certified scrap streams
  • ✓ Upgrade to modular, "Buy-It-For-Life" designs

Mission & Circular Vision

Why REFIX?

Modern consumer culture pushes an unsustainable default narrative: if an object is damaged or unused, throw it away and buy a new one. This fuels planned obsolescence, depletes virgin resources, and fills municipal landfills.

REFIX inverts this dynamic. By evaluating items across all material domains—not just electrical appliances—we democratize circular decision-making, celebrate the Right-to-Repair, and make sustainable choices economically obvious.

📖 Algorithmic Methodology & Benchmarks

1. Repairability Score Heuristic

Score = (MaterialEase × 0.30) + (SeverityScore × 0.35) + (PartsAvailability × 0.20) + (UserSkill × 0.15)
Scores above 55% indicate strong viability for home or community restoration.

2. Economic Viability Ratio (EVR)

EVR = RepairCost / ReplacementCost
• EVR ≤ 0.50: Highly favorable repair zone.
• 0.50 < EVR ≤ 0.70: Secondary consideration (depends on sentimental value and age).
• EVR > 0.70: Triggers REUSE or REPLACE pathway.

3. Safety & Toxicity Veto Rules

Certain materials trigger an immediate REPLACE veto regardless of cost:
• Damaged PTFE / Teflon cookware (toxic fluoropolymer ingestion risk)
• Fractured impact helmet EPS foam (permanent structural shock loss)
• Swollen lithium-ion batteries (thermal runaway fire hazard)

4. Carbon & Water Savings Benchmarks

Derived from life-cycle analysis (LCA) data per kilogram of material avoided (e.g. 15 kg CO₂e/kg denim cotton, 45 kg CO₂e/kg modular electronics, 1.8 kg CO₂e/kg solid wood).
